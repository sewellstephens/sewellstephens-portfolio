About me

I have 10 years of experience building for the web and 6 years of experience in SaaS. I originally got into web development because I liked designing and creating things. Before I started building online, I drew pictures on paper and wrote stories. I never got particularly good at drawing, but that creative interest stuck with me — I just found that I preferred designing things digitally.

I’m the founder of Ranktune, an AI visibility platform that helps businesses get mentioned in AI search. We track how AI models talk about a brand, identify visibility gaps, and turn those insights into content and clear next steps.

I also founded Anchorclick, a done-for-you marketing partner. I build systems across content, SEO, paid ads, email, and SMS that help businesses get found, follow up with prospects, and win more customers — without having to piece every channel together themselves.
